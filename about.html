The Job Title

I started with words, learning how to research, write, edit and communicate ideas clearly. Over the years, that foundation expanded into website content, SEO, social media, content planning, campaigns, performance tracking and digital marketing, mainly in the B2B technology space.

I’ve worked closely with marketing, sales, design and product teams, taking projects from planning to execution. My work has usually been end-to-end, from creating and publishing content to optimising it, promoting it and checking how it performs.

Over time, my work has grown into content strategy, GTM, demand generation, performance marketing and technology. Today, I work where content, business and technology meet, while building deeper expertise in AI and emerging intelligent systems.
