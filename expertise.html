FAQs

Questions clients often ask

What does a content strategist do?

A content strategist connects business goals, audience needs, positioning and distribution into a clear system for what a brand should publish, why it should publish it and how performance should be measured.

How do SEO and AI search fit together?

Traditional SEO still depends on relevance, usefulness, technical accessibility and authority. AI search adds another layer: content needs clear entities, direct answers, strong context and information that can be reliably interpreted and cited.

Do you work with B2B SaaS and technology companies?

Yes. My experience is concentrated around B2B technology, SaaS, AI, enterprise products and marketing technology, where buyers often need substantial education before conversion.

Can you develop a complete content strategy?

Yes. A typical engagement can cover audience and search research, positioning, content architecture, editorial planning, production guidance, optimisation, distribution and measurement.

What is AEO and GEO?

AEO, or Answer Engine Optimisation, focuses on making content useful for answer-oriented search experiences. GEO, or Generative Engine Optimisation, focuses on improving how a brand and its information can be understood and surfaced in generative search experiences.
